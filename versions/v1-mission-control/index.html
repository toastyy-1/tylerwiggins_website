Statement of work

I build the systems that let rockets be tested, flown, and understood: data acquisition and control on the test stand, telemetry in flight, and the guidance math in between.

I study aerospace engineering at the University of Tennessee. Most of my work happens with the Student Space Technology Association (SSTA). I advise on electronics and rocketry subsystems there, and I co-lead command and control for a liquid rocket built to reach 70,000+ ft.

Outside the club I write simulation software. My current project is a 6-DoF guidance and dynamics simulator in C++, which SSTA now uses to verify and validate its thrust vector control system.
