Projects

Big Liquid Command & Control

SSTA · Aug 2025 – Present · Co-lead

The command-and-control platform for SSTA’s liquid-fueled rocket, which targets a 70,000+ ft apogee. It reads the sensors on the test stand, fires the triggers, and records the data used to judge engine performance.

  • Built DAQ infrastructure controlling 8 sensors and 6 triggers
  • Led recovery and analysis of thrust and pressure data
  • Helped write the test-fire and launch procedures behind 5+ successful test fires

Orbital Trajectory & Flight Control Sim

Personal project · Apr 2026 – Present · C++

A general-purpose 6-DoF rocket guidance and dynamics simulator. SSTA selected it to verify and validate its thrust vector control system.

  • RK4 integration with J2 gravity perturbations; passes all NASA NESC test cases
  • Staged, Lambert-based guidance that steers a rocket between two points on Earth

GLASS

SSTA · May 2026 – Present · Hardware design, team lead

A hardware-in-the-loop system that replays recorded test-fire data into the DAQ. The DAQ can be tested against real hot-fire signals without propellant on the stand.

  • Designed a 3-board PCB assembly with an 8-channel DAC emulating load cells and pressure transducers
  • Voltage reproduction within 0.02% error at 50+ Hz
  • Lead the 10-person team building the test and verification software

PHASE Flight Controller

SSTA · Jan – May 2026 · Firmware & telemetry

A flight telemetry device for a 70,000+ ft apogee rocket.

  • Implemented a custom LoRa protocol for two-way flight computer / ground station communication
  • Built a data collection and digital twin system for real-time flight prediction

Regenerative Rocket Engine

SSTA · Aug 2025 – Present · Fabrication

A custom-designed, regeneratively cooled liquid rocket engine.

  • Formulated a nickel sulfamate electroforming process to build the outer shell on the copper combustion chamber
  • Provided guidance on the electroforming procedure, safety guidelines, and system components
